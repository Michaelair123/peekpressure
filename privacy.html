Service scope and cleaning-only business

PEEK PRESSURE provides exterior cleaning services only. Our services are limited to cleaning activities such as pressure washing, related exterior surface cleaning, and interior or exterior vehicle cleaning within the scope we agree with the customer. We do not provide construction, remodeling, alteration, repair, replacement, installation, painting, coating, sealing, resurfacing, landscaping, roofing, electrical, plumbing, structural work, or other property-improvement or modification services.

Nothing in this Privacy Policy, our website, a quote, or a customer communication is intended to represent that PEEK PRESSURE performs or is licensed to perform work that requires a contractor license, permit, registration, or other credential that we do not hold. If a requested project falls outside our cleaning-only scope or requires credentials we do not have, we may decline the request or refer the customer to an appropriately qualified provider.

What we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect: identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number and account or session identifiers; commercial information such as requested services, quote or booking details and transaction information; internet or electronic activity information such as website interactions; approximate property or service information; and other information you voluntarily provide, including messages and photos.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, understand cleaning requests, prepare or discuss quotes, schedule and provide services, communicate with customers, maintain security, prevent abuse and spam, operate our website and systems, keep limited customer context when enabled, and comply with legal obligations.

Lucy, our AI assistant

Our website uses Lucy, an AI assistant, to answer service questions and help collect leads. Lucy may use limited service-related context previously provided by a customer so the customer does not have to repeat basic details. Customer memory is limited to service context such as name, contact information, service, location, property type, approximate size, surface, condition and timing. We do not intentionally use customer memory to make decisions about credit, employment, housing, insurance, education, or other similarly significant matters.

We do not intend to retain full chat transcripts as customer memory. Please do not provide passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers, health information, or other sensitive personal information through Lucy, email, or text.

Sources and disclosures

We collect information directly from you through the website, Lucy, email, text, phone, scheduling tools and other communications. We may disclose information to service providers and contractors that help us operate website hosting, AI assistance, email, scheduling, security, communications and lead handling. We do not sell personal information for money. We do not knowingly share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, legal obligations, dispute resolution, security, accounting and business records. Limited Lucy customer memory may be retained for up to 180 days unless deleted earlier. Other records may be retained longer where reasonably necessary for the service relationship or legal requirements.

Cookies and similar technology

Our site may use essential cookies or similar technologies to maintain a secure session and support site functionality. We do not use cookies for the purpose of selling personal information.
