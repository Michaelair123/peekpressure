What we collect

When you contact PEEK PRESSURE, we may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, property address, requested service, property or surface details, approximate job size, timing, photos, and messages.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to inquiries, understand cleaning requests, prepare or discuss quotes, schedule services, provide services, communicate with you, and operate and improve our website and customer service.

Lucy, our AI assistant

Our website uses Lucy, an AI assistant, to help answer questions and collect service-related lead information. Lucy may use limited service context you previously provided so you do not have to repeat yourself. We do not intend to store full chat transcripts as customer memory.

What we do not want you to send

Please do not send passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information through Lucy, email, or text.

Service providers

We may use technology providers to operate our website, communications, scheduling, AI assistance, and lead handling. Information is shared only as reasonably necessary to provide or operate those services.

Retention and security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. Limited service-related customer memory may be retained for up to 180 days. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about the personal information we have associated with your inquiry or to request deletion, subject to applicable legal or operational requirements.
